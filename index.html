Why fat matters: CoQ10, D3, K2, Omega-3, Resveratrol, Curcumin, and Fisetin are all fat-soluble. Without dietary fat at the same meal, absorption drops 50–80%. You only need ~10–15g of fat — a full high-fat meal is not required.
💡 Sinclair's approach: Mix resveratrol into full-fat Greek yogurt each morning. The yogurt provides adequate fat, adds probiotics, and is easy to prepare. Use it as your standard morning supplement vehicle for all fat-soluble supplements.
⚠️ Avoid grapefruit — it's a potent CYP3A4 inhibitor that would amplify enzyme inhibition from berberine, quercetin, and resveratrol, raising prescription drug levels unpredictably.
